How to install

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap the button above. Chrome asks you to confirm the download.

  2. 2

    Allow installs from this source

    Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, tap Settings and enable “Allow from this source” for Chrome.

  3. 3

    Tap Install

    Android may warn that the app is not from Play Store. That is normal for a direct APK — tap Install anyway.

  4. 4

    Choose your language

    Pick once on first launch. The app remembers it and always opens in that language.
